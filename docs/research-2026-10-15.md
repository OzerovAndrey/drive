# Automotive UX research — 15 October 2026

Read public product pages and comparison flows; this is content/interaction research, not a claim of visual browser testing.

| Source | Observed pattern | DRIVE adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| [Polestar 4 overview](https://www.polestar.com/de/polestar-4-models/polestar-4-coupe/) | Secondary model navigation, interior/material detail, gallery, charging and specifications | Sticky model sections, gallery, cabin hotspots, specification categories and electric content |
| [Porsche model overview](https://www.porsche.com/international/models/) | Powertrain/body filters, technical data, standard equipment, comparison and configuration | More practical card data, standard equipment, downloadable spec sheet and expanded comparison rows |
| [Porsche configurator](https://models.porsche.com/de-DE/model-start) | Shared selection and configuration journey with model-specific technical information | Preserve current saved selections and configuration while enriching model detail |
| [Carwow comparison](https://www.carwow.co.uk/car-comparison) | Explain the significance of differences, not only numeric rows; acknowledge ownership context | Best-fit / trade-off notes, practical buying guides and explicit energy-cost assumptions |

## Implementation decisions

Keep three fictional brands and two themes on one dataset. Add detail where it helps selection. Do not invent user reviews, ratings, certifications, dealer inventory or financing offers. Standard equipment/specifications are fictional concept data with visible notices. Shared interior imagery is explicitly labeled as a collection design study.

Energy comparison is an editable, local, energy-only calculator. It does not calculate total ownership cost or promise savings. Charging simulation uses battery energy divided by a constant selected power; real charging curves/losses are excluded and clearly explained.

## Sources of facts vs creative content

The reference sites inform the page structure. None of their manufacturer-specific numbers, commercial offers or brand assets are copied into DRIVE. DRIVE model data and photography are fictional creative content. No real dealership contact or transaction is introduced.
